Nurturing Life Through Ancient Wisdom

At SriMaata, we guide expectant mothers on a sacred journey of Garbha Samskara — blending Ayurveda, rituals, thoughts, and mindful practices to nurture not just the body, but the mind and soul of the baby.

Rooted in ancient Indian texts, our care begins before birth, shaping emotional, intellectual, and spiritual wellbeing from the womb itself.

About SriMaata

Sri Maata - The Divine Mother

Sri Maata is not just a name. It is a prayer, a presence, and the spirit behind everything we offer here. This space is inspired by Madurai Meenakshi Amman, revered as Sri Maata, the Universal Mother.

She embodies strength, nurturing, wisdom, and divine femininity. Her image reminds us that every woman carries within her the power to create, protect, and uplift life.

Dr. Lakshmi N. Prasad

Dr. Lakshmi N. Prasad is an Ayurvedic doctor and antenatal care guide who offers a compassionate and conscious approach to pregnancy.

She believes pregnancy is not a condition to be treated, but a sacred journey to be honoured. Through Garbha Samskara, she helps women deepen their connection with their body, their baby, and their inner self.
